My current research is focused on using machine learning approaches to better understand how brains are built, using both neuroimaging and cellular-level data. I have prior experience with molecular techniques, electrophysiology, behavioural scoring, psycholinguistics, and survey creation.

For a full list of publications, see [my Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hVbH4RUAAAAJ).
